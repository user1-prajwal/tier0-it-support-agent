# Employee offboarding checklist

## Before the final working day

This checklist is for authorized HR and designated managers coordinating an employee departure. Departure details are confidential and should be shared only with people who need them to complete an approved task. HR owns the official departure record and confirms the employee's final working day, employment status, manager, and any special handling instructions.

The manager should identify active projects, business-critical responsibilities, and colleagues who will receive the work handover. Agree on a handover date and record the location of business documents in approved company storage. Do not ask the departing employee to move company information into a personal email account, personal cloud drive, or removable device.

HR should confirm whether the departure is planned, immediate, or subject to a separate instruction. The timing of account changes must follow the approved HR and security workflow. Do not share a departure date broadly before the communication plan is approved. If the employee has company equipment, arrange a return appointment and provide the approved return instructions.

## Equipment and access coordination

The manager records company assets assigned to the employee, such as a laptop, access card, security key, mobile device, or other equipment. IT uses the approved asset inventory to confirm ownership and return status. Record missing or damaged equipment through the normal asset process rather than making informal deductions or promises.

HR sends the confirmed departure details to the designated IT access team through the approved restricted workflow. The request should identify the employee's work account and effective date, but should not include passwords or authentication codes. IT determines the appropriate timing for disabling accounts, revoking sessions, and transferring ownership of business resources. Managers must not ask colleagues to use the departing employee's account.

If the employee owns shared mailboxes, calendars, repositories, or business applications, the manager should identify the business owner who will receive access through the normal approval process. Personal files and business records must be handled according to the organization's retention and privacy rules.

## Final day and post-departure review

On the final working day, HR confirms completion of the required exit steps and records any outstanding items. The manager confirms that the handover is complete or documents the remaining work and its new owner. IT confirms that access actions were completed according to the approved request and records exceptions for follow-up.

If equipment has not been returned, HR and the manager follow the established reminder and escalation process. Do not attempt to locate or access a personal device without the required authorization. Any concern about data removal, unusual downloads, or suspected misuse must be referred to the security team for assessment.

Store the completed checklist in the restricted HR record. Limit access to authorized HR personnel and approved reviewers. The record should contain completion status, dates, responsible owners, and outstanding actions, but not passwords, recovery codes, or unnecessary personal details.
