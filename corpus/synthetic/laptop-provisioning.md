# Laptop provisioning

## Requesting a device

A manager or approved onboarding contact requests a laptop through the internal service portal.

The request should state the employee's start date, work location, role, and any approved hardware requirements.

Employees should not purchase a replacement device and submit it as a company-managed laptop without prior authorization.

## Standard preparation

The endpoint team assigns an asset record, installs the approved operating system image, and enrolls the device in management.

The standard configuration includes endpoint protection, disk encryption, update policies, and the approved software catalogue.

The employee receives the device only after the enrollment checks have completed.

## First sign-in

At first startup, connect to a trusted internet connection and follow the setup instructions supplied with the device.

Sign in using the employee's own work identity. Complete the required multi-factor prompt and allow management policies to finish applying before installing optional tools.

Do not share the initial sign-in link or verification code with another person.

## Asset identification

The training inventory example uses asset tag LT-4826.

A real device has its own printed asset tag and inventory record.

Include that tag when reporting damage, loss, or a hardware problem.

Do not publish the tag in a public forum or include it in an unrelated support request.

## Moving or returning a laptop

Employees must notify the endpoint team before transferring a laptop to another person or location.

The device may need a configuration change or inventory update.

When a laptop is returned, follow the approved handover process and do not attempt to erase it independently.

## Lost, stolen, or damaged devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop promptly through the security reporting route so the organization can protect its data and accounts.

For physical damage, stop using the device if continued operation could cause further damage and contact the helpdesk.

Do not open the chassis or replace internal components unless authorized support instructs you to do so.
