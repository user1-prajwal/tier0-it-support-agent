# Service status FAQ

## Where to check service health

The public service status page provides general information about the availability of supported online services.

It is intended for customers and visitors who need to know whether a published service issue is being investigated.

The page does not display private account details, internal network information, or employee-only maintenance notes.

## Understanding status labels

Operational means that the service is currently reported as available.

Monitoring means that a change has been made and the service is being observed for stability.

Degraded performance means that some users may experience slower responses or limited functionality.

Partial outage means that a portion of the service is unavailable while other functions may continue to work.

## Incident updates

A status notice may include the affected service, the time the issue was first observed, and the latest investigation update.

Updates are published when verified information becomes available.

The absence of a new update does not necessarily mean that investigation has stopped.

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance notices are published when a service change is expected to affect availability.

The notice may include a maintenance window and a summary of the expected impact.

Times are displayed with the time zone stated on the page.

Users should save their work before a published maintenance window begins.

## What the page cannot confirm

A general status notice cannot confirm whether a particular person's account, device, or local internet connection is working correctly.

If the status page reports normal operation but one user still has a problem, that user should follow the relevant account or device support process.

## Reporting a public-facing issue

Visitors can use the contact route shown on the public website to report a problem with a public service.

Include the page or service name, approximate time, and a short description.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, private documents, or authentication codes through a general feedback form.
