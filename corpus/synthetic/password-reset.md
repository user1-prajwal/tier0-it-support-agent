# Password reset

## Self-service steps

1. Open your organization's approved password reset portal from a trusted browser or device.
2. Enter your registered work email address or employee username.
3. Complete identity verification using a registered method, such as an authenticator application, registered phone number, or recovery email.
4. Once verified, choose the option to reset your password.
5. Create a new password that meets the organization's requirements. Avoid using your name, employee ID, previously used passwords, or easily guessed information.
6. Confirm the new password and submit the request.
7. Wait for the confirmation message, then wait approximately one minute before attempting to sign in again.

If you are signed in on multiple devices, some applications may continue using an existing session. Sign out and sign in again if an application asks you to authenticate.

## When self-service does not work

If you do not receive a verification code, check that your registered phone number or recovery email is correct. Check your message filtering settings and wait briefly before requesting another code. Avoid repeatedly requesting codes because this may trigger temporary verification limits.

If your registered verification method is unavailable, do not attempt to bypass identity verification. Contact the IT helpdesk through an approved support channel and request assistance with account recovery.

If the portal reports that your account is locked, wait for the indicated lockout period if one is provided. If the account remains inaccessible, contact the IT helpdesk. Do not repeatedly attempt to sign in with an incorrect password.

If the password reset succeeds but you still cannot sign in, verify that you are using the correct work account and the newly created password. If the issue continues, report the error message and the application you are trying to access to IT support.

## Security precautions

Never share your password, verification code, or recovery link with another person, including someone claiming to be an IT support employee. IT support should never need your current password or one-time verification code.

If you suspect that someone else has accessed your account, reset your password immediately if possible and report the suspected compromise to the IT security team. Do not reuse the suspected compromised password.
