# HR policy exceptions

## Purpose and confidentiality

This document describes a fictional process for reviewing exceptions to standard people policies.

It is intended only for authorized human resources personnel.

Employee-specific circumstances and supporting records must remain in approved confidential HR systems and must not be copied into general IT tickets.

## Exception reference

The synthetic policy identifier is HRX-2026-08.

It applies to the training example for a temporary work-location exception.

This identifier is invented and does not represent a real employer policy.

HR staff must verify the current policy version and jurisdiction before advising on an actual case.

## Submitting a request

An employee submits an exception request through the approved HR channel and explains the requested change, the intended duration, and the work-related reason.

The employee should provide only the information necessary for the review.

Medical or other sensitive supporting records must be handled through the designated confidential process rather than attached to an ordinary email.

## Review responsibilities

The assigned HR partner checks that the request is complete and identifies the policy owner who must review it.

The manager may provide operational context but should not receive confidential supporting material unless access is authorized and necessary.

A decision must be recorded by the person or group with delegated authority.

## Decision and communication

The decision record should state whether the exception is approved, declined, or requires more information.

If approved, it should include the scope, start date, end date, and any conditions.

The employee receives the outcome through the approved HR channel.

A manager receives only the information needed to plan work.

## Review and expiry

Temporary exceptions should be reviewed before their end date.

HR confirms whether the original reason still applies and whether an extension is permitted.

An extension requires a new documented decision; an earlier approval should not be treated as indefinite permission.

## Record handling

Store the request and decision in the designated HR record system with access limited to authorized personnel.

Do not place confidential case details in shared team folders or public project trackers.

If information is sent to the wrong recipient, follow the HR privacy incident process promptly.
